About Titanz Zenuva General Security Guard Services

Titanz Zenuva General Security Guard Services L.L.C is a UAE-based security services provider and a subsidiary of Velqor Group, committed to delivering professional, reliable, and SIRA-compliant security solutions across the UAE. Backed by a trained and licensed workforce, we provide manned physical guarding, event security, office support, cleaning, and lifeguard services tailored to the unique needs of our clients. As part of Velqor Group, we combine strong local expertise with structured operational standards, ensuring every service is delivered with integrity, accountability, and excellence. We see security not just as a service, but as a responsibility built on trust and long-term partnerships.
