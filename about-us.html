About Titanz General Security Services

Your Trusted Partner for Comprehensive Security & Facility Solutions in UAE

Leading Security Services Provider in UAE Since 2010

Titanz General Security Services L.L.C. is a premier SIRA-approved security company headquartered in Dubai, providing comprehensive security solutions, professional cleaning services, and certified lifeguard services across the United Arab Emirates.

With over a decade of excellence in the security industry, we've built our reputation on unwavering commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction. Our team of highly trained professionals serves residential communities, commercial establishments, industrial facilities, and hospitality venues throughout Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and the Northern Emirates.

As part of the Velqor Group of Companies, we leverage extensive resources, cutting-edge technology, and industry best practices to deliver security and facility management solutions that exceed client expectations.
